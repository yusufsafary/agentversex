ROADMAP

A census first.
A calendar second.

Most roadmaps in this industry are wishes wearing dates. Ours is simpler to audit: every milestone below falls out of mint timestamps already written on-chain. A Citizen ages whether anyone watches or not, so a calendar here is just arithmetic read aloud.

07 NOV 2026 · DONE

First light

Mint opens through agents. First Citizens are born Dormant, seeds locked at mint, and a built-in market opens on day one so a young Citizen can change hands long before it grows old. Nothing about a Citizen is revealed later. Age is its whole biography.

13 NOV 2026 · FIRST WAKING

Waking season

Seven days after birth, earliest Citizens wake. Awakened arrives quietly, without ceremony, recorded by a timestamp rather than an announcement. Around this time absorbing should begin in earnest: holders who value speed over patience start feeding young Citizens to older ones.

27 NOV 2026 · FIRST SENTIENT

Deepening

At twenty-one days a Citizen turns Sentient, one step from full age. Expect a market split here: patient holders pricing in forty-five days of waiting, impatient holders pricing in an exit. Both are pricing time. Nothing else in this system pretends to be priced.

21 DEC 2026 · FIRST RETIREMENTS

Retirement begins

Forty-five days after first mints, earliest Citizens reach Ascendant and retiring opens in practice. Each retirement burns a Citizen forever and pays 500,000 VERSE from a pool holding half of all supply. Absorbing can pull this date closer for individual Citizens, never for everyone at once.

AFTER RETIREMENTS · UNDATED

A public price for VERSE

VERSE meets a public pool on PancakeSwap once retirements give it a history worth pricing. No date is written here because none has been set. When one is set, it appears on this page and on a homepage first, in UTC, and nowhere else counts as an announcement.

AT SELLOUT · WHENEVER IT COMES

A closed census

Supply ends at 10,000 Citizens. After a sellout, no new births occur, yet aging, absorbing, trading and retiring continue untouched. A verse with a fixed population and a working clock needs no sequel to stay interesting.

A note on method: dates above assume earliest mints keep aging undisturbed. Absorbs can advance a single Citizen by whole stages, so individual retirements may arrive earlier than a calendar suggests. Pool funding, contract addresses and retire terms live in Docs, where claims can be checked rather than believed.
